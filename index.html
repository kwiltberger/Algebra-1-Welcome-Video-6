Introduction Week

How to Complete a Prove It!

Demonstrate what you understand by showing your work, explaining your reasoning, and following every direction.

Purpose of Prove It!

This is where you demonstrate mastery.

🧠 Understand Show that you understand the week’s learning targets.
✏️ Explain Show your reasoning instead of giving only final answers.
✅ Complete Include every required task and submit it correctly.
1

Read the Entire Assignment First

Do not begin after reading only the first question. Review the overview, directions, required tasks, submission format, checklist, and rubric.

What Must You Do?

Identify every problem, explanation, recording, or response that must be completed.

How Must You Submit?

Check whether the assignment requires a video, file upload, text response, or live demonstration.

What Resources Are Allowed?

Follow the directions about notes, calculators, Desmos, reference sheets, or other tools.

How Will It Be Graded?

Review the checklist or rubric before beginning so you know what complete work looks like.

Submission Requirements

Follow the format listed on your assignment.

🎥 Video or Screen Recording Show your work clearly and explain your thinking aloud.
📄 Written Submission Upload readable work with all steps and explanations included.
💻 Live Demonstration Be prepared to solve problems and explain your reasoning live.

Show Mathematical Reasoning

Do more than state the answer.

1. Show Work Include the important calculations and steps.
2. Name the Strategy Identify the method or rule you used.
3. Explain Why Describe why your method works for the problem.
4. Check Explain how you know the answer is reasonable.
A final answer without supporting work or explanation may not fully demonstrate mastery.
🎙️ Recording Tips
2

Make Your Recording Easy to Follow

  • Make sure your work is visible.
  • Speak clearly and loudly enough to hear.
  • Explain each important step.
  • Keep the camera or screen focused on the task.
  • Review the recording before uploading it.

Before Submitting

Use the checklist or rubric.

Every Task Confirm that no required problem or explanation was skipped.
Readable Work Make sure written work, images, and recordings are clear.
Correct Format Submit the required file, recording, or response type.
Your Prove It! should show your own understanding. Do not copy another student’s work or simply repeat an example.

Not Ready Yet?

Review before you submit.

Ready Complete and submit Prove It! when you can explain the learning targets independently.
Need Review Return to Learn It!, Practice It!, or Self-Check when you need another example.
Need Support Schedule a Teacher Check-In when you still have questions or need guided practice.
